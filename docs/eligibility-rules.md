# Eligibility Rules

## Property Type

| Question | Answers | Effect on Eligibility |
|----------|---------|----------------------|
| What type of property do you have? | Detached, Semi-detached, Terraced, Flat, Bungalow | All eligible. Flat properties proceed through the form but may have different support options available. |

## Ownership

| Question | Answers | Effect on Eligibility |
|----------|---------|----------------------|
| What is your ownership status? | Own outright, Mortgage, Shared ownership, Rent | **Own outright**: Eligible. **Mortgage**: Eligible. **Shared ownership**: Eligible if all parties agree. **Rent**: Eligible if landlord consent obtained. |

## Household Income Band

| Question | Answers | Effect on Eligibility |
|----------|---------|----------------------|
| What is your household income? | Under £12,570, £12,571 - £50,270, £50,271 - £125,140, £125,141+ | All income bands eligible (hybrid approach). Income determines support generosity/level, not eligibility. Lower income households receive more generous support. |

## Existing Insulation

| Question | Answers | Effect on Eligibility |
|----------|---------|----------------------|
| Which insulation do you have? (select all that apply) | Loft insulation, Cavity wall insulation, Solid wall insulation, Floor insulation, None of the above | Determines eligibility for insulation grants independently. All users are eligible based on insulation status. |

## Current Heating

| Question | Answers | Effect on Eligibility |
|----------|---------|----------------------|
| What is your current heating system? | Gas boiler, Oil boiler, Electric heating, Heat pump, Other | **Heat pump**: Ineligible for heat pump grant (already have one). **All others**: Eligible for heat pump grant. Heating type does not determine available upgrades. Heating eligibility is independent of insulation status. |

## Prior Grant History

| Rule | Effect on Eligibility |
|------|----------------------|
| If applicant has already received a grant for a specific grant type | Cannot reapply for the same type of grant. Applicants can only receive one grant per grant type. |
